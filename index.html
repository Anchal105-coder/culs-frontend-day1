    Questions

    Can I pick the herbs?

    Yes, a few leaves for your kitchen. Please do not pull out whole plants.

    Can I bring my dog?

    Yes, on a lead. Keep it out of the beds.

    Why are there so many bees?

    Thyme, lavender and sage flower all summer, and bees love them.
